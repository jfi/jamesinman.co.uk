You can contact me at [james@jamesinman.co.uk](mailto:james@jamesinman.co.uk).

## What data this site collects

This site is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

### Analytics

This site uses [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io) and [Simple Analytics](https://www.simpleanalytics.com) — both privacy-friendly analytics tools that do not use cookies, do not track you across websites, and do not collect personal data. No visitor IP addresses are stored, and no unique identifiers are created.

You can review Simple Analytics' data practices in their [what we collect](https://docs.simpleanalytics.com/what-we-collect) documentation, and Plausible's in their [data policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy).

### Cookies

This site does not set any cookies.

### Fonts and external resources

All fonts and icons used on this site are self-hosted — no requests are made to Google Fonts, CDNs, or other third-party services when you browse pages.

### Contact and booking

If you book an introduction call via [TidyCal](https://tidycal.com) or send an email, the data you provide will be processed by those respective services. Please refer to their own privacy policies for details.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data I hold about you. Since this site collects minimal data, there is unlikely to be anything to retrieve — but if you have any concerns, please get in touch at [james@jamesinman.co.uk](mailto:james@jamesinman.co.uk).

## Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
